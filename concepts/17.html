04 / Less mystery

Big feelings.
Small details.

What happens first?

You get in touch with a question or a brief introduction. The counsellor can explain availability and the next step, so you can decide whether to continue.

What if I don’t know what to say?

“I’m not sure where to begin” is a beginning. You can ask about his approach before deciding whether you would like a session.

Where would we meet?

Vancouver, BC. The exact office location and any online session options will be added once confirmed.

Fees, time & the practical bits

Session fees, length, insurance eligibility and cancellation terms need to be confirmed by the counsellor before this site is published.

Who is this practice for?

His verified areas of practice, client age groups and professional designation will be listed here. This concept does not assume a clinical specialty.
